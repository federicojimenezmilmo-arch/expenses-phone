    Settings

    Your data file

    The browser keeps a working copy, but the file is the real record. Save after importing or reviewing; load it to pick up where you left off on another browser or after clearing browser data.

    Phone sync

    Every change is encrypted with your passphrase and pushed to your secret gist a couple of seconds later; the phone page reads it back. The token and passphrase live in this browser only, never in your save file.

    The phone page is this app with editing removed and no data inside; put it on your GitHub Pages site and open that address on the phone. Upload it again whenever this file changes. The offline snapshot has today’s data baked in and needs no connection.

    Cards and accounts

    Money sent from the bank account to either card is recognized by the card’s name here, so keep these matching how they appear on the bank statement.

    Expense categories

      Income categories

      Money coming in that is really a refund of an expense can be given an expense category instead; it then reduces that category rather than counting as income.

        Your rules

        A rule assigns a category to every transaction whose description contains the text, optionally only when money is going out or coming in. Your rules run before the built-in ones, top to bottom. Changing rules re-categorizes auto-categorized transactions; confirmed ones stay as they are.

        Transfers by person

        Everyone a SPEI has gone to or come from, read from the statement lines. Pick a category and every transfer with that person in that direction takes it, now and on future imports. The concept text, when the bank includes it, is shown underneath to help you recognize who is who.
